Main Story

Resident Evil is a world all about exploration and secrets. In Resident Evil Requiem, you can discover many new areas throughout the Rhodes Care Center, Raccoon City and ARK, filled with dark secrets and items that are essential for survival against the horror within. To 100% complete Resident Evil Requiem, you can complete a playthrough with all files collected, all Mr. Raccoons discovered, all Safes opened for Grace, and all BSAA containers opened for Leon. It is actually recommended to start your first game on Standard (Classic) Difficulty so that you unlock all other difficulty-related trophies automatically with this one.

Head to the Wrenwood Hotel

File 1 / 75

Wrenwood City Guide
Walk down the rainy street until you see a poster of a map on your right. Interact with it to read and obtain the file.

Investigate the crime scene

File 2 / 75

Investigation Report
As soon as you enter the Hotel's Lounge & Bar area, the game will prompt you to press to open the Items Menu and select the file already in your inventory. Examine it and interact with the photo sticking out of the folder to read the file.

Old Key
In the Lounge & Bar area, there is a photo taped to a pole on the right side of the bar. Interact with it and examine it to find a key attached to the back.

Go to Room 204

File 3 / 75

Hotel Pamphlet
As soon as you enter the Hotel's Lobby, you can find the pamphlet on the floor in front of you near the luggage cart.

File 4 / 75

Notice of Closure
In the Office, open the desk drawer to find the notice inside.

Wrenwood Hotel Map
In the Office, look to the wall on the right of the desk to see the map.

Wire Cutters
On the Second Floor in the Rec Room, look in the back corner to find a toolbox with screwdrivers sitting on the pool table.
